Identity-based public-key encryption (IBE), its mathematical constructions, key-generation trust model, and security foundations.

Research direction

Encryption from identity

In identity-based encryption, an identity string can serve as a public identifier for encryption, while a trusted private-key generator issues the corresponding decryption key. This changes how public keys are identified and introduces important questions about trust and key generation.

My interests cover the design and security analysis of public-key cryptographic schemes, including identity-based constructions and their protocol-level applications.

Areas of interest

  • Identity-based encryption schemes and their security models
  • Key extraction, trusted authorities, and escrow implications
  • Connections between public-key primitives and secure protocols
