Featured manuscript

Dijkstra Is NOT Greedy

A Global-to-Local Proof of Correctness

The paper presents a global-to-local correctness proof for Dijkstra’s algorithm. Starting with the full set of paths to unresolved vertices, it uses a three-step exclusion argument to determine the global shortest path through frontier candidates and relaxation.

The revised manuscript, version 14, also discusses strictly positive and nonnegative edge weights, equal-length shortest paths, and the relation between the path-minimization argument and the standard algorithm.

Current manuscript: version 14 (October 2026). The full bilingual article is available on the dedicated project website.
